6. DEFECT FOUND BY THE NEW TESTS (fixed in this checkpoint)
----------------------------------------------------------
findProgressTemplateError used string.includes on a percent-sign string
with the neighbouring character, and "".includes is always true, so a
template ending or starting with {percent} was wrongly refused. Fixed
with a Set lookup; covered by the accepted-template test.
