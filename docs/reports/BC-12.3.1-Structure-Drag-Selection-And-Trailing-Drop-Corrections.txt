BC-12.3.1 — Structure Drag Selection & End-of-Container Drop Corrections
=========================================================================
Date: 2026-11-22
Milestone: BC-12 (Structure Panel Drag-and-Drop Ordering)
Status: implemented, focused validation passed, NOT committed (waiting for ChatGPT QA)
Baseline: BC-12.3 is committed (5f6c309). BC-12.4 was not started.


1. SUMMARY
----------
Correction A — multi-selection is preserved:
- Dragging one row of a multi-selection moves only that element.
- Every selected element stays selected, and the moved one becomes
  primary.
- Cancel and refused drops keep the selection too.
- While verifying, a related real bug was found and fixed: Escape (to
  cancel a Structure drag) also triggered the Canvas's "Escape clears the
  selection" shortcut.
Correction B — explicit end-of-container drops:
- The gap under a container's last row now resolves by the pointer's
  indentation, like an outliner: the end of that container, or the end of
  an ancestor.
- The gap under an OPEN container's row now means "before its first child"
  (before, it meant "after the whole subtree", a different place from
  where the line was drawn).
- While a row is dragged, a "Drop here to move to the end of the page" area
  appears under the last row.
Everything goes through the same validateMove / moveElement, so the rules,
no-op handling and one Undo step per move are unchanged.
Recommendation: APPROVE BC-12.3.1 and close BC-12.3.


2. ROOT CAUSES
--------------
A1. The store's moveElement always set selection = [moved element], the
    contract shared with Canvas drags and Alt+Arrow. The Structure drag
    used that contract unchanged.
A2. (Found live) The Canvas's window-level Escape handler
    (CanvasInteractionBoundary) clears the selection unless the event is
    already defaultPrevented. dnd-kit's PointerSensor cancels a drag on
    Escape without marking the event, so Escape-cancel also cleared the
    selection.
B1. The drop target came only from the hovered row:
    - the gap under a last child could only mean "after that row";
    - there was nothing under the final row of the tree.
B2. Under an open container's row, the "after" band meant "after the
    container", i.e. after its whole subtree. But the line was drawn
    directly above the container's first child: the indicator and the
    result disagreed.


3. CORRECTIONS
--------------
A. Selection:
   - moveElement(nodeId, targetId, zone, { keepSelection: true }), used only
     by the Structure pointer drag: the selection becomes
     createSelection(previous ids + moved id, filtered to existing ids),
     with the moved id as primary.
   - Without the option (Canvas drag, Alt+Arrow), the moved element is the
     only selection, unchanged.
   - Pick-up:
     - an unselected row becomes the plain selection (unchanged
       BC-10/BC-12.3 rule);
     - a selected row leaves the selection as it is.
   - Escape: while a Structure drag is active, a capture-phase window
     keydown listener marks Escape as handled (preventDefault). dnd-kit
     still cancels the drag, and the Canvas shortcut skips it. The listener
     exists only during the drag and is module-level, so the same function
     is added and removed.
B. Targets (structureDragDrop.ts):
   - trailingLevelCandidates(doc, rowId, draggedId): [row, parent if row is
     its last child, grandparent if the parent is ITS last child, …], up to
     the page's own children. The dragged element doesn't count when
     deciding "last", unless it is the row itself. "After candidate X" is
     exactly "append to the end of X's parent".
   - resolveStructureDropTarget, "after" band:
     - an open container with children → before its first child;
     - otherwise the candidate whose row indentation starts at or left of
       the pointer, deepest first (left of everything: the shallowest).
   - The target carries levelId, the row whose indentation the line starts
     at, so the drawn line shows the level it lands at.
   - resolvePageEndTarget: the page-end area → inside the page root
     (appended last).
   - Indicator: a line is drawn at the gap's y (the top or bottom of the
     hovered row, or the top of the page-end area), starting at that
     level's label indent. The "inside" box is unchanged.

5. SELECTION BEHAVIOR
---------------------
Each case below is verified in Chromium (test 9) and/or the store tests.
- A, B, C selected; drag B before A:
  - only B moves;
  - A, B and C stay selected ("3 selected");
  - B is primary and its row is focused.
- Undo / Redo restore the order. The selection stays A, B, C (history
  snapshots hold the selection, as before) and never points at a missing
  element.
- Escape mid-drag: no change, "3 selected" kept (was cleared before the
  fix).
- A refused drop (into a List): no change, "3 selected" kept.
- A drag of an unselected row (the Title): it becomes the only selection
  (plain selection), and then moves.
- A refused or no-op move with keepSelection leaves the selection object
  untouched, and records no history.
- Canvas and Alt+Arrow moves still select only the moved element.
- BC-10 multi-selection semantics (Ctrl/Cmd toggle, Shift range, count)
  are unchanged.


6. END-OF-CONTAINER TARGETING
-----------------------------
Example tree: Section B > Inner > Deep, followed by List. In the gap under
"Deep":
- at Deep's indentation (or to the right) → after Deep = the end of Inner;
- at Inner's indentation → after Inner = the end of Section B;
- at Section B's indentation or further left → after Section B (page
  level, before List).
Destination ownership comes from the real hierarchy and the rows' actual
padding, so it also holds for depths past the 6-level indent cap. A
candidate appears only where its row is visually the last one in that
spot, so a trailing target can never append to the wrong ancestor.
An open container's own row: its "after" band is "before its first child".
"After the container itself" is reached from the gap under its last
descendant at the container's indentation. There is no ambiguity between
"append to the parent" and "insert after the parent".
Collapsed containers: their rows have no visible children, so the "after"
band means after the container, as before. "Inside" still appends to the
end (and auto-expands after 600 ms).
Empty containers: "inside" (the middle band) appends; tested with an empty
Flow box.
Page end: the dashed area (40px, full tree width, inside the Structure
panel only) appears only while a row is dragged. It appends to the page
root.


7. STRUCTURAL VALIDATION
------------------------
Every resolved target, including ancestor levels and the page end, is
validated by validateMove on hover and applied by moveElement → planMove on
drop. Tested:
- invalid nesting, a list item at an ancestor level or at the page end:
  "That place can't contain this kind of element";
- cycles: Section B at the level of its own Inner → "descendant";
- Navbar / Pagination / Free-bounds rules: the same validator (BC-12.2's 36
  store tests, re-run).
A refused drop changes nothing and shows the shared notice.


8. UNDO/REDO
------------
- One drop = one history entry: three trailing drops plus the page-end
  append, with Undo/Redo checked in Chromium.
- A no-op append (the page's last child dropped on the page end again) is
  announced "Alpha was not moved: it is already there." and records
  nothing: the next Undo still undoes the earlier page-end append.
- Refused drops record nothing.

10. REMAINING LIMITATIONS
-------------------------
- The level under a last child depends on the pointer's horizontal
  position. Since the grip is on the right of the row, reaching an
  ancestor level means moving the pointer left over the indentation. This
  is standard outliner behaviour; the line shows the level.
- The page-end area adds 44px under the tree only while dragging (a
  reflow at drag start, below all rows).
- Escape during a CANVAS dnd-kit move drag still clears the selection.
  That predates this checkpoint and is outside the Structure scope; raised,
  not changed.
- Touch and pen: Chromium CDP input only, as in BC-12.3.
- Keyboard refusals are still announced by both the Structure region and
  the notice banner (BC-12.2).
