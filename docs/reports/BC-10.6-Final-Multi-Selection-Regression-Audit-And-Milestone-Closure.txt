1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------
The audit exercised BC-10's features TOGETHER: selection, effective
Mixed/Unknown, shared edits and Reset, clipboard, Free placement, HTML ids,
Navbar/Pagination invariants, interactions, Undo/Redo, Preview/Export and
save/reopen. It found ONE genuine BC-10 regression, and fixed it.

R1 (Medium, introduced in BC-10.5.1, FIXED)
  Copies from two DIFFERENT Free containers were laid out in one shared
  coordinate space. Elements at the same stored position in their own
  containers (e.g. both at 20,20) were pasted exactly on top of each other.
  That contradicts the existing placement rule: one insertion never lands
  on top of another.
  Fix: offsets are kept only among copies from the SAME source container;
  sub-groups from different containers are stacked top to bottom in
  document order, 16px apart. That is the stacking rule BC-10.5.1 already
  used for Flow-origin copies, so no new policy was invented.
  Alternative for the owner (not implemented): place the sub-groups side by
  side instead of stacked.

No other defects found.
- All 20 approved product decisions hold (section 4).
- Focused tests pass: 268 Vitest tests in 20 BC-10 files, 22 Chromium
  tests, and 14 Firefox + WebKit tests.
- No unresolved High or Critical BC-10 defect.
Recommendation: BC-10 is ready for closure review. The closure decision is
ChatGPT's.

20. KNOWN LIMITATIONS AND DEFERRED IMPROVEMENTS (non-blocking)
--------------------------------------------------------------
- Owner decision invited: Free sub-groups from different containers are
  stacked vertically. Side-by-side placement is the alternative.
- Free bounds are the existing approximate one-shot bounds (stored size,
  else 400 × 300). A group that doesn't fit them is refused, never shrunk.
- After a paste the copies are selected, so if one of them is a container
  the next paste goes inside it (the existing single-paste rule).
- BC-10.4: the full uniform editors require the same tag. Size, Shape and
  Media fit stay bulk. Flow/Free switching is one container at a time.
  Transform/Transition have no per-field Reset while they differ. Bulk
  numbers are px only.
- Existing projects with duplicate authored ids are not repaired (the
  export still warns).
- Multi-drag, multi-resize and responsive breakpoints remain deferred, as
  approved.
